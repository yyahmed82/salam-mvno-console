WHAT HAPPENED
From the moment HyperPay became the only gateway, every STC Pay payment was declined: 26,094 attempts, 0 successes. HyperPay returned result code 800.100.156 — “transaction declined (format error)” on 26,091 of them (100.0%): the request was rejected at the acquirer/connector configuration before the customer ever saw the STC Pay OTP screen, so from the customer's side the wallet simply “did not work”. The other HyperPay rails were healthy in the same window (mada / Visa / Mastercard: 39,734 successful payments, 24.2% declined — ordinary customer declines, not a zero-success failure), which confirms the problem was specific to the STC Pay integration on HyperPay and not to HyperPay as a whole. The peak was Friday 04 September with 4,504 declined attempts from 1,068 customers.
CUSTOMER IMPACT
5,233 customers tried to pay with STC Pay and were declined; 3,129 of them retried three times or more (customers averaged 5.0 attempts each), which is what drove the call-centre load. 3,981 customers (76.1%) eventually paid with another method (mada, card or UPG after the re-enable). 1,252 customers (23.9%) have no successful payment on record after their failure — this is the group we recommend Marketing / Care reach out to. By platform: Android 22,578, Web 2,156, iOS 1,360 attempts. Recharge was by far the most affected purpose.

BASELINE BEFORE THE CHANGE
In the 7 days before the cutover (27 Aug → 3 Sep 16:27 KSA) UPG processed 125,425 payments with 63,542 successes and a 15.0% decline rate — ordinary customer declines (insufficient balance, wrong OTP), not a gateway fault. HyperPay carried very little customer traffic before 3 Sep (a few hundred payments on 31 Aug), so the STC Pay rail on HyperPay had not been exercised at production volume before the switch.
ACTIONS TAKEN AND NEXT STEPS
Done: UPG re-enabled on Thursday 10 September 2026 18:10 KSA, restoring STC Pay for customers through UPG; HyperPay kept for cards. Open with HyperPay: root cause and fix of the STC Pay connector configuration (result code 800.100.156), with a written confirmation and a controlled test before STC Pay is ever routed to HyperPay again. Proposed: (1) targeted communication / recharge offer to the 1,252 customers not recovered; (2) a per-rail payment alert on the Operations Console — any rail with a high attempt volume and zero successes over 15 minutes pages Digital Operations, so a silent failure of this kind is caught within minutes rather than through call-centre volume; (3) any future gateway switch to run as a phased ramp (5% → 25% → 100%) with per-rail success checks at each step.

The Cases sheet in the workbook lists all 26,094 attempts individually; full customer identifiers can be provided on request through the Operations Console. I remain available to walk through the numbers.
