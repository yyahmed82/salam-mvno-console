Mobile · money owed to a customer after a payment the platform did not deliver on (activation, port-in, plan change, SIM / eSIM replacement, delivery, duplicate capture). Left to right is time; each lane is who or what holds the case. Red boxes are where the old cycle lost days; purple boxes are what the console and the Agent 2 refund desk do on their own.

Old Mail-driven cycle — until 25 Sep 2026

Nothing watched the platform: the case existed only once the customer complained. L2 rebuilt the facts by hand from proxycms and the database, typed them into a mail, waited for an approval reply, posted the refund, and no register kept what was decided or why.

New Detected, reviewed, batched, reconciled — alpha.98 → alpha.100 (26 Sep 2026)

The console reads the replica every 15 minutes and lists the case with its evidence before anyone complains. The refund desk (Agent 2) reviews it — rules first, the on-prem model when the rules are unsure — assigns it to the Mobile L2 team, mails the new cases, sends the daily approval request with the payment ids L2 needs, opens an incident that the ack SLA chases, and closes the case from the proxycms register itself. Every step is recorded; the reasons and the misses feed the RCA and the next detectors.
