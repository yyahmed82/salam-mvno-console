Dear team,

Following our session and yesterday's note on the agreed enhancements, I've added two things to the console that are ready for you to use now.

**1) One place for all your remarks — "Tickets & feedback"**

From today, please stop keeping suggestions and issues in side chats or notes and instead **raise them directly in the console** so nothing is lost and everything is tracked with a timestamp and owner.

How to raise one (any of you, any role):
- Click the **"?"** icon (top-right) → **"Raise a ticket"** — or, if you're on the Tickets board, the green **"＋ New ticket"** button.
- Choose **Suggestion** (an enhancement / new idea) or **Issue** (something broken or wrong).
- Add a short **title**, a **description**, and **attach screenshots** (up to 4) — screenshots help us a lot.
- Submit → you'll get a **ticket reference** (e.g. TKT-000123) on screen and a **confirmation email** that it's under evaluation.

Each ticket then moves through **Open → Under evaluation → In progress → Closed / Rejected**, and you'll be notified as the status changes. Please funnel *all* your review remarks, big or small, through this — it becomes our single backlog.

*(Screenshot attached: raise-a-ticket.png)*

**2) The "L2 Workbench" — your follow-up hub**

I've also added an **L2 Workbench** (in the gear ⚙ menu, under User management) built specifically for L2 follow-up work. Four tabs:

- **Activity** — per-user view of what each of us did in the console (pages, timeline drill-downs, searches, response times) from the audit trail — useful for reviewing how the tool is actually being used.
- **Incident replay** — pick a past window and see what alerts fired then; good for post-mortems and training.
- **Test / what-if** — safely test an alert rule against a historic window, fire a test alert to check notification routing, and run synthetic health checks — a bench for validating before it matters live.
- **Docs hub** — upload runbooks / specs; anything shared here also **feeds Yusr's knowledge base**, so uploaded docs become answerable in the chat.
